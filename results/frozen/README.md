# Frozen compact results

This directory stores compact numerical products used directly in `sample.tex` and the manuscript result sections.

The large raw synthetic tables are generated by the experiment scripts and are not duplicated in repository history. The committed frozen products are exact compact summaries of the completed runs.

Baseline equal-width/full-sky products:
- `experiment_integrity.csv`: final row counts, sky counts, convergence/validity, and transit ranges.
- `full_sky_dr4_beta_separation_summary.csv`: all-sky paired Delta-chi2 summaries over the final native beta/separation grid.
- `native_photocentre_bias_a1.csv`: pooled photocentre parameter biases at a/sigma=1.
- `native_vs_matched_n53_boundary_counts.csv`: counts of sky positions at each sampled native and matched first-crossing boundary.
- `native_vs_matched_n53_summary.csv`: exact headline native-versus-matched numerical and correlation summary.

Response-fidelity products:
- `response_fidelity_integrity.csv`: row counts, convergence, scientific validity, and multi-peak counts for the 720-fit, 270-fit, and 100-seed experiments.
- `response_fidelity_central_biases.csv`: central M0/M1/M2 bias table at beta_G=0.25 and a/alpha=1 for both tested elongations.
- `response_fidelity_beta025_primary_mass_bias.csv`: primary-mass figure data versus a/alpha at beta_G=0.25 and beta_PSF/alpha=1.5.
- `response_fidelity_beta025_paired_dchi2.csv`: paired ten-seed M0-M2 and M1-M2 mismatch summaries over the beta_G=0.25 response-fidelity slice.
- `external_information_control_summary.csv`: 30-seed strong/medium/weak external-orbit control, grouped by fitted model.
- `matched_m2_100seed_summary.csv`: high-statistics matched-M2 estimator check.

The response-fidelity products are generated by:
- `scripts/run_response_fidelity.py`
- `scripts/run_external_information_control.py`
- `scripts/run_matched_m2_control.py`

All widths and angular ratios in these files belong to idealized research surrogates. They must not be interpreted as calibrated Gaia PLSF widths or physical Gaia resolution thresholds. The nominal scanning-law schedules are likewise not actual source-level observation lists.

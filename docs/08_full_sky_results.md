# 08 — Full-sky baseline results

The native-schedule equal-width baseline contains 23,000 fits over 46 nominal sky positions.

At the representative configuration \(\beta_G=0.25\) and \(a_{\rm rel,ang}/\sigma=1\), the median paired photocentre-minus-resolution-aware mismatch over sky positions is approximately

\[
\mathrm{median}\,\Delta\chi^2 \simeq 2195.39,
\]

with a range of roughly 834.14–8766.64. The Spearman correlation with native transit count is about 0.7709.

This strong count dependence motivated the matched-N control. The result is interpreted only as evidence that the accumulated detectability of a response mismatch depends strongly on Gaia-like sampling. It is **not** a calibrated physical Gaia separation threshold.

Compact manuscript-support products are frozen under `results/frozen/`, and the project figures under `figures/` are generated from those summaries.

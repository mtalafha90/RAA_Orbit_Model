# Native full-sky versus matched-N=53 result

This note freezes the final comparison between the 46-position nominal-DR4 full-sky experiment and the matched-transit-count control. All numbers below are recomputed from the completed `sky_position_bias.csv`, `matched_n53_bias.csv`, and their scan-geometry tables.

## Integrity

| quantity | native full sky | matched N=53 |
|---|---:|---:|
| fit records | 23,000 | 11,040 |
| paired realizations | 11,500 | 5,520 |
| sky positions | 46 | 46 |
| Gaia transits | 53--310 | exactly 53 |
| optimizer success | 100% | 100% |
| `scientific_valid` | 100% | 100% |
| maximum multi-peak epochs flagged | 0 | 0 |

The final grids therefore remain entirely in the single-peak domain of the current Gaussian response surrogate.

## Light-fraction result

In the native full-sky experiment, the largest median paired
`Delta chi2 = chi2_photocentre - chi2_resolution_aware`
occurs at `beta_G = 0.25` for all 46 sky positions and all five tested `a/sigma` values: **230/230 sky/separation combinations**.

The matched control compares `beta_G = 0.05, 0.25, 0.45`; `beta_G = 0.25` is again the maximum at all 46 positions and all four tested separations: **184/184 combinations**.

## Native sky dependence

At `beta_G = 0.25` and `a/sigma = 1`:

- sky minimum median Delta chi2 = **834.1408**;
- sky median median Delta chi2 = **2195.3908**;
- sky maximum median Delta chi2 = **8766.6390**.

The descriptive `Q16(Delta chi2) > 0` first-crossing boundary occurs at `a/sigma = 0.40` for 41/46 sky positions and at 0.50 for 5/46.

The all-ten-seeds-positive boundary first occurs at:

- 0.40 for 18/46 positions;
- 0.50 for 23/46;
- 0.60 for 5/46.

At the same physical point, the Spearman correlations across sky position are:

- Delta chi2 versus native transit count: **+0.770868**;
- Delta chi2 versus maximum directional scan-angle gap: approximately **-0.640**;
- Delta chi2 versus normalized directional entropy: approximately **+0.358**.

These are descriptive correlations, not causal estimates by themselves.

## Matched-N=53 control

At the same `beta_G = 0.25`, `a/sigma = 1` point:

- sky minimum median Delta chi2 = **300.0384**;
- sky median median Delta chi2 = **1180.9445**;
- sky maximum median Delta chi2 = **2230.2622**.

The ratio of the matched and native sky medians is **0.537920**.

After equalizing the number of observations, the correlation with the *native* transit count becomes **-0.003208**, effectively removing the native monotonic count dependence.

Residual matched-schedule correlations remain:

- Delta chi2 versus matched normalized scan-angle entropy: **+0.339943**;
- Delta chi2 versus matched maximum directional gap: **-0.274622**.

Thus scan geometry still contributes after the dominant count effect is controlled.

## Approximate count scaling

Define

`r_chi = Delta chi2_N53 / Delta chi2_native`

and

`f_N = 53 / N_native`.

Across the 46 positions,

- Spearman rho(r_chi, f_N) = **0.923253**;
- median(r_chi / f_N) = **1.013826**;
- median native Delta chi2 per transit = **21.4442**;
- median matched Delta chi2 per transit = **22.2820**.

For this orbit, noise model, surrogate response, and strong-response point, the discrimination therefore accumulates approximately linearly with transit count. This is **not** claimed as a universal Gaia scaling law.

## Transition shift under matched N

For `beta_G = 0.25`, the matched `Q16(Delta chi2) > 0` boundary first occurs at:

- 0.40 for 31/46 positions;
- 0.50 for 15/46.

The matched all-seeds-positive boundary first occurs at:

- 0.40 for 8/46;
- 0.50 for 24/46;
- 0.60 for 13/46;
- 1.00 for 1/46.

Additional native observations therefore move the descriptive transition inward for a substantial fraction of the sky.

## Photocentre parameter bias

At native `a/sigma = 1`, pooling all sky positions and seeds, the photocentre model has median fractional `beta_G` biases of:

- -22.6% at beta_G = 0.05;
- -18.0% at 0.15;
- -12.6% at 0.25;
- -7.17% at 0.35;
- -2.12% at 0.45.

For beta_G = 0.25, the pooled median biases are approximately +0.51% in parallax, -0.61% in M1, and -0.66% in M2. The light-fraction bias can therefore be appreciable even when the independent resolved astrometry and SB2 data largely protect the dynamical masses.

## Reproduction

The committed comparison CLI reproduces the frozen tables and control figures:

```bash
python scripts/compare_native_matched_n.py \
  results/sky_position_dr4_full/sky_position_bias.csv \
  results/sky_position_dr4_full_matched_n53/matched_n53_bias.csv \
  results/sky_position_dr4_full/scan_geometry.csv \
  results/sky_position_dr4_full_matched_n53/matched_n53_scan_geometry.csv \
  --output-dir results/native_vs_matched_n53
```

Compact frozen tables used by the manuscript are stored under `results/frozen/`. The full 23,000-row and 11,040-row raw synthetic tables are intentionally not duplicated into the repository history; they are generated by the documented experiment runners.

## Interpretation boundary

All numerical transition values refer to the equal-width Gaussian research surrogate. `sigma_response_mas` is not a calibrated Gaia LSF width, the nominal GOST schedules are not actual source-level observation lists, and `Delta chi2` is used as a paired descriptive model-comparison quantity rather than a Gaussian-significance statistic.
